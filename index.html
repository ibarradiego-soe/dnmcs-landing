How it works

Invite the table. Then you have the documents.

There is no daily product to log into. The work is one sitting per person, then the reports.

  1. 01

    Invite the people who sit at the table

    Co-founders, the C-suite, the pair that keeps missing each other. You send the invites. This is for the team you already have — not a screen for candidates.

  2. 02

    Each person consents, then completes

    Nobody is assessed by surprise. After an explicit yes, they complete the assessment in private. Raw answers stay private. They are never part of a teammate’s view.

  3. 03

    Cards and pair reports appear

    Each person gets a Working With Me card. Strategic pairs — CEO and CMO, co-founders — get a report. The team gets a one-page dynamics map. Later hires can be onboarded on the same plan.

  4. 04

    Use them before the next hard conversation

    Open the documents before the hire, the raise, or the conversation you have been postponing. The point is to walk in already knowing how the room decides.

Example · fictional team

Working With Me card

Maya Chen

CEO · Northwind Sample Co.

How I decide

I close fast when I have a customer signal. I stall when the risk is a person, not a market. If you need a no from me, bring it as a recommendation, not a tour of options.

How to work with me

Put the ask in the first sentence. I will hear dissent after a direction is on the page, not before. Do not send a long memo the night before a board meeting and expect a careful no.

Under pressure

I narrow the choices and pick. That keeps the company moving. It can also skip the person who needed another day to say what they actually think.

What I need from the room

Someone who will name the people-risk I am treating as a timing problem. Write it down. I will engage with a sentence I can agree or refuse.

Privacy and consent

Raw answers stay private. Sharing is a yes, not a default.

This only works if people can be honest. So the assessment is private, and nothing derived is visible to teammates until the person has previewed it and agreed.

Never shared

Your raw answers. Not with the CEO. Not with the team. Not inside a pair report or the team map. Teammates do not get a window into what you typed.

What can be shared

Authorized conclusions only: your Working With Me card, pair reports, and the team map — after you preview exactly what will be shown.

Who sees it

Only the teammates you authorize for that card or report. There is no company-wide feed of assessments.

If the scope changes

A new audience or a wider share needs a new yes. The old consent does not stretch to cover it.

FAQ

The questions a careful CEO actually asks.

How long does this take?

Each person completes one assessment in a single sitting. There is nothing to do every day after that. The value is the documents, used when the next hard conversation arrives.

Will my CEO see my raw answers?

No. Raw answers are never shared. The CEO — and anyone else — sees only the derived cards and reports you have previewed and authorized.

Is this MBTI?

No. Dnmcs does not assign you a type. It produces documents about how this specific team decides under pressure: how you work, how a pair collides, and how the room is put together.

Who can see my card?

Only the teammates you authorize. You preview what will be shared. If the audience or the scope changes, you will be asked to consent again.

Is this used to hire or fire people?

No. Dnmcs is not a screening tool and not a performance score. It is for people who already sit at the table — or who have already been hired onto it.

What about people we hire later?

New members can be onboarded on the same annual plan. They consent, complete the assessment, and their card and reports update the map.

What language is the product in?

English only. Every customer-facing string is written in English.
